# Code of conduct

This is a small project about making a TV work better for the people who live with it. Take part in that spirit.

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Be specific and patient with newcomers. Most people here are fixing their own TV, not writing software for a living.
- Assume good faith, and say plainly what you did and did not check.
- Do not post other people's private information, including IP addresses, account names and keys.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block people who break these rules. To raise a concern, open an issue that does not name the person, or use the repository's private reporting option if it is enabled.
