# Using a phone as the TV remote

For when the remote is lost or a button (such as volume down) does not work. Tested by the owner on 2026-11-03: it worked on a Pixel and on an iPhone, including volume.

The TV must be on and connected to the network, and the phone must be on the same Wi-Fi network as the TV. Set it up once per phone.

## Android phone

1. Open the **Google TV** app.
2. Near the bottom right, tap **TVs nearby**.
3. Choose the TV.
4. Type the code shown on the TV screen and tap **Pair**.
5. Open the remote in the app. Optional: add the remote to the phone's Quick Settings to open it from any screen.

Source: Google, "Use your phone as a remote" (support.google.com/googletv/answer/11136134).

## iPhone

1. Open the **Google Home** app. It must be signed in to the same Google account as the TV.
2. Tap **Devices**, then **Add**, then **Set up device**, then **New device**.
3. Choose the TV and follow the prompts.
4. Back in **Devices**, choose the TV and tap **Remote**.

Source: TCL, "Control your TCL Google TV with the Google Home app" (support.tcl.com/en_US/control-your-tcl-google-tv-with-the-google-home-app).

## If the TV does not appear

- Check the phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the TV, and that the app is the latest version.
- Close and reopen the app. On Android, restart the phone if that does not help.
- Power on and off may not work from the phone. Switch the TV on with the physical remote if needed.
